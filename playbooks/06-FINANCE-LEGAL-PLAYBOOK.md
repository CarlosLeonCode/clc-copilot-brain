# Finance & Legal Governance Playbook

## 1. Objectives & Scope

The Finance & Legal Brain (`finance_legal/`) safeguards enterprise value, ensures fiscal discipline, maintains pricing consistency, and enforces regulatory and contractual hygiene.

```
finance_legal/
├── pricing/        # Pricing tiers, packaging models, discount approval matrices
└── compliance/     # Data privacy policies, standard NDA templates, contract terms
```

---

## 2. Pricing Governance & Discount Authorization Matrix

Discounts erode enterprise valuation if unmanaged. Sales teams must operate strictly within the authorized discount matrix in `finance_legal/pricing/pricing-tiers-and-discount-matrix.md`.

### Approval Thresholds:
- **0% – 10% Discount**: AE Discretion (with multi-year commitment or upfront payment).
- **11% – 20% Discount**: Sales Director Approval.
- **21% – 30% Discount**: VP of Sales / CRO Approval.
- **>30% or Custom Payment Terms**: VP of Sales + CFO / Finance Director Approval.

---

## 3. Contract & NDA Protocols

- **Mutual NDA**: Standard terms in `finance_legal/compliance/mutual-nda-template.md` are pre-approved for immediate execution by sales or partnership leads.
- **Redlines**: Any prospect or vendor requesting material changes to liability, IP indemnity, or governing law requires legal review.
- **Record Retention**: Executed agreements are archived in the secure vendor/customer drive with metadata recorded in the brain.

---

## 4. Compliance & Privacy Audits

- **SOC2 / ISO 27001 / GDPR / CCPA**:
  - Annual re-certification schedules.
  - Quarterly review of third-party vendor data processors.
  - Continuous logging of security policies and data retention rules.
