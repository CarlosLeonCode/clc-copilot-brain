## 2. Discount Approval Governance Matrix

Discounts reduce long-term contract valuation and set damaging precedent. Strictly adhere to the following approval thresholds:

| Discount Percentage | Required Justification | Required Approver | Contractual Concession Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0% – 10%** | Annual upfront payment | Account Executive (AE) | 12-month prepay required |
| **11% – 20%** | Multi-year agreement or high-growth logo | Sales Director | 24-month minimum commitment |
| **21% – 30%** | Public case study, logo rights, joint webinar | VP of Sales / CRO | Customer case study & quote within 60 days |
| **> 30%** | Exceptional strategic alliance | VP of Sales + CFO | Board/Executive approval and 3-year upfront commitment |
