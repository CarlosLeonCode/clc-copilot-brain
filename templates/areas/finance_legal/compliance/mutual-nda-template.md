# Standard Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (MNDA)

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date of execution by and between:

**Party A**: {{COMPANY_NAME}}, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Discloser/Recipient"), and  
**Party B**: ____________________________ ("Discloser/Recipient").

### 1. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" refers to any proprietary information, technical data, architecture blueprints, trade secrets, customer battlecards, pricing matrices, source code, or know-how disclosed by either party to the other, either directly or indirectly in writing, orally, or by inspection of tangible objects.

### 2. Obligations of Receiving Party
The receiving party agrees:
- To protect confidential information using the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of like nature, but no less than reasonable care.
- Not to disclose any Confidential Information to third parties except to its employees, contractors, and legal advisors who have a need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as those herein.
- Not to reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble any software or prototypes provided.

### 3. Term and Termination
This Agreement and the obligations herein shall remain in effect for a period of three (3) years from the date of disclosure.
