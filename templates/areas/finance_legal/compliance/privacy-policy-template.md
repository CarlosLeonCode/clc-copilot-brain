# Data Privacy Policy & Compliance Standards

> **Last Updated:** 2026-09-30  
> **Applies to:** All company products, CLI tools, and customer engagements

## 1. Zero Data Egress Principles
We believe institutional knowledge is the most sensitive asset an organization owns.
- The Second Brain engine is architected as a local-first system.
- Code repositories, Markdown notes, schemas, and credentials are never transmitted to unapproved third-party servers.
- When local AI tools run against repository files, data retention is subject to the developer's explicitly configured local model or private enterprise endpoint.

---

## 2. Customer Data Retention & Deletion
- Customer telemetry (if enabled) is strictly anonymized and opt-in.
- Customers may request a full export or immediate hard-deletion of any cloud-synchronized records within 30 days under GDPR and CCPA compliance obligations.
