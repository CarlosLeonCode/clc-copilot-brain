# Sales Enablement Hub

Welcome to the Sales area of the Organizational Second Brain. This directory is the single source of truth for revenue enablement, competitive intelligence, and customer acquisition playbooks.

## Structure
- [icps/](./icps/): Ideal Customer Profiles detailing demographics, pain points, triggers, and decision criteria.
- [battlecards/](./battlecards/): Field-tested competitor intelligence with strengths, weaknesses, and silver bullets.
- [objections/](./objections/): Root-cause objection matrices and conversational pivot scripts.
- [scripts/](./scripts/): Structured outreach, discovery call questions, and closing frameworks.
- [case-studies/](./case-studies/): Metric-backed proof points and customer stories.

## Governance
- **Review Cadence**: Bi-weekly.
- **Playbook**: Refer to [01-SALES-PLAYBOOK.md](../../playbooks/01-SALES-PLAYBOOK.md).
