# ICP: Enterprise CTO / VP of Engineering

## 1. Profile Overview
- **Role Titles**: Chief Technology Officer, VP of Engineering, Head of Architecture
- **Company Stage**: Series B to Enterprise (50 - 2,000 engineers)
- **Primary Objective**: Accelerate developer velocity while safeguarding system stability, compliance, and IP privacy.

---

## 2. Core Pain Points
1. **Context Fragmentation**: Engineers lose 4+ hours per week searching for outdated documentation, scattered across Notion, Confluence, Slack, and Google Docs.
2. **AI Copilot Hallucinations**: Generic AI coding assistants write code inconsistent with internal APIs, security rules, and architectural standards.
3. **Onboarding Friction**: New senior hires take 90+ days to become net-positive contributors due to tribal knowledge silos.

---

## 3. Buying Triggers
- Security / SOC2 audit upcoming requiring centralized documentation of engineering policies.
- Migration to modern AI IDEs (Cursor, Windsurf, Claude Code) failing due to lack of ground truth context files.
- Restructuring or rapid engineering team expansion (>25% headcount growth in 6 months).

---

## 4. Decision & Evaluation Criteria
- **Security & Data Sovereignty**: Must support local/git-based files without sending sensitive repository data to third-party proprietary clouds.
- **Developer Adoption**: Zero friction CLI and Markdown-first workflow that engineers respect.
- **Time to Value**: Measurable reduction in onboarding time within 30 days.

---

## 5. Disqualification Signals
- Organizations without dedicated engineering leadership or Git-centric workflows.
- Teams exclusively seeking closed, proprietary SaaS wikis with no AI integration appetite.
