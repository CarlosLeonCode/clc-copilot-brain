# Objection Matrix: Pricing & Budget Constraints

> **Category:** Pricing  
> **Severity:** High  
> **Schema Definition:** [pricing-too-high.json](./pricing-too-high.json)

---

## 1. What the Prospect Says (Trigger Phrases)
- *"We don't have budget for another tooling subscription this quarter."*
- *"Why pay for a Second Brain framework when we can just write markdown files ourselves?"*
- *"Our CFO has frozen all new SaaS vendor onboarding."*

---

## 2. Root Cause Analysis
The prospect perceives the Second Brain as an incremental cost rather than a cost-reduction asset. They compare it against "free" unmanaged documentation, ignoring the massive productivity leak of stale specs and AI hallucination debugging.

---

## 3. The 2-Step Conversational Pivot Script
1. **Validate and De-escalate**:
   > *"Understood, [Name]. CFOs across tech are scrutinizing every line item right now, and that's exactly why our enterprise partners adopt our model."*
2. **Refocus on Developer Cost**:
   > *"If two senior engineers spend just 3 hours a week debugging bad code generated by ungrounded AI copilots, that's already costing you $3,000 every single month. Would you be open to seeing our 3-week ROI calculation?"*

---

## 4. Key Counter-Arguments & Evidence
- **Real Engineering Hours**: 4+ hours saved per engineer per week.
- **Onboarding Velocity**: Cuts ramp time by 60% for new hires.
