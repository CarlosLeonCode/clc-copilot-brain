## 1. Executive Snapshot
LegacyDocs Cloud is an incumbent enterprise wiki provider. They target centralized IT departments with closed SaaS hosting, per-seat licensing, and proprietary AI add-ons.

---

## 2. Strengths vs. Weaknesses

| Competitor Strengths | Their Core Weaknesses |
| :--- | :--- |
| Huge enterprise installed base & FedRAMP | Siloed outside Git repositories; not developer friendly |
| Familiar non-technical WYSIWYG editor | No native grounding for Cursor, Windsurf, or Claude Code |
| Deep enterprise sales & procurement ties | Aggressive pricing ($25–$45/user/month) with vendor lock-in |

---

## 3. Our Silver Bullets (How We Win)
1. **Git-Native Single Source of Truth**: Knowledge lives in Markdown and JSON inside the repo, evolving through pull requests alongside the codebase.
2. **Universal AI Agent Grounding**: Pre-configured multi-IDE adapters (`CLAUDE.md`, `.cursorrules`, `.windsurfrules`) ensure zero AI hallucinations.
3. **Open Architecture & Zero Seat Tax**: No lock-in, extensible schemas, and fast local CLI auditing.
