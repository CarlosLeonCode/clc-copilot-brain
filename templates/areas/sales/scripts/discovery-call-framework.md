# Sales Discovery Call Framework

## Objective
Qualify technical fit, identify quantifiable business pain, and isolate decision criteria for adopting the Second Brain architecture.

---

## Stage 1: Setting the Agenda (2 minutes)
> *"Thanks for making time today, [Name]. The goal of our 25 minutes is to understand how your engineering and operations teams currently maintain institutional knowledge, see if our Second Brain engine matches your workflow, and decide whether a proof-of-concept makes sense. Does that sound fair?"*

---

## Stage 2: Discovery & Diagnosis (15 minutes)

### Diagnosing Developer Context & AI Tooling:
1. *"What percentage of your engineering team is actively using AI code assistants like Cursor, Copilot, or Claude Code?"*
2. *"How do these tools know your internal architecture and business rules—are developers manually copy-pasting context into chat prompts?"*
3. *"When an engineer leaves or changes squads, how much of their context leaves with them?"*

### Diagnosing Financial & Operational Impact:
4. *"How long does it take a newly hired senior engineer to ship their first major production PR?"*
5. *"Have you calculated the engineering cost of stale documentation or failed compliance audits?"*

---

## Stage 3: Demonstrating the Transformation (5 minutes)
Show the side-by-side comparison:
- **Without Second Brain**: Fragmented cloud wikis, copilot hallucinating outdated functions, broken symlinks.
- **With Second Brain**: Version-controlled repo SSOT (`AGENTS.md`), CLI doctor audits (`copilot-brain doctor`), zero setup friction.

---

## Stage 4: Next Steps & Closing (3 minutes)
> *"Based on what you shared about onboarding lag and AI hallucination, running our CLI doctor on a pilot repository would show immediate gaps. Are you free next Tuesday at 2 PM to run a live 15-minute repository audit?"*
