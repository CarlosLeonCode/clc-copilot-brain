## Days 1 – 30: Absorb & Align
- [ ] Complete local dev environment setup and pass CLI verification (`copilot-brain doctor`).
- [ ] Read the complete company handbook in `hr_people/handbook/`.
- [ ] Shadow at least 3 customer discovery or feedback calls with Sales/CS.
- [ ] Ship first minor bug fix or documentation improvement pull request in week 2.
- [ ] 30-day retro with manager to review early feedback and goals.

---

## Days 31 – 60: Contribute & Collaborate
- [ ] Own and ship an end-to-end feature slice from approved PRD in `product/specs/`.
- [ ] Participate as primary code reviewer on at least 5 team pull requests.
- [ ] Lead one internal sprint demo or engineering brown-bag session.
- [ ] 60-day milestone check-in on project deliverables.

---

## Days 61 – 90: Own & Innovate
- [ ] Take full on-call rotation shift (supported by secondary buddy).
- [ ] Propose and author an Architecture Decision Record (ADR) or SOP enhancement.
- [ ] Onboard your first new team member or act as an onboarding buddy.
- [ ] 90-day comprehensive performance and trajectory review.
