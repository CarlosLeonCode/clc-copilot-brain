# Company Handbook & Operating Culture

## 1. Operating Manifesto & Core Values

### Bias for Action & Asynchronous Communication
- Written memos in pull requests beat unrecorded 60-minute meetings.
- Default to public Git repos, issue trackers, and shared channels over private DMs.

### High Trust & High Accountability
- We evaluate output, architectural elegance, and business impact—not hours spent sitting in front of a webcam.
- Everyone has full visibility into company roadmaps and repository documentation.

---

## 2. Remote Work Norms & Collaboration
- **Core Collaboration Hours**: 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM EST for scheduled syncs; flexible outside this window.
- **Deep Work Blocks**: Tuesdays and Thursdays are designated focus blocks with no internal recurring meetings.
- **Expense Reimbursement**: $1,000 home office setup budget and $150/month remote wellness stipend.

---

## 3. Time Off & Leave Policies
- **Flexible PTO**: We encourage a minimum of 20 days off annually.
- **Parental Leave**: 16 weeks fully paid leave for all primary and secondary caregivers.
- **Sick & Mental Health**: Take what you need to recover without doctor notes.
