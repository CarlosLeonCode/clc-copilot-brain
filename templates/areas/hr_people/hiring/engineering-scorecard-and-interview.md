# Role Scorecard: Senior Full-Stack / Systems Engineer

## 1. Role Mission
Build scalable, resilient backend services and developer tooling while maintaining deterministic schemas and high test coverage across our core platforms.

---

## 2. Key 12-Month Outcomes
1. **Velocity**: Independently ship features from PRD to production deployment with <0.1% defect rate.
2. **Architecture**: Author at least 3 approved ADRs addressing system bottlenecks or technical debt.
3. **Enablement**: Mentor junior engineers and contribute directly to updating the engineering Second Brain.

---

## 3. Evaluation Competencies & Question Bank

### Competency 1: Systems Thinking & Clean Architecture (Score 1–5)
- *Question*: "Walk us through a technical architecture you designed that had to handle unexpected load spikes or breaking upstream schema changes. How did you isolate failures?"

### Competency 2: Asynchronous & Written Communication (Score 1–5)
- *Question*: "Describe a situation where you had a strong disagreement with a peer or tech lead over an implementation detail. How did you resolve it asynchronously in code reviews?"

### Competency 3: Pragmatism & Velocity (Score 1–5)
- *Question*: "How do you decide when to take on technical debt to hit a critical market window versus when to halt feature development to refactor?"
