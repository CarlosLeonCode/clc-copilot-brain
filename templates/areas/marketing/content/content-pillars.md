# Marketing Content Pillars

All marketing, technical articles, and social commentary must anchor into one of these four core pillars:

## Pillar 1: AI Grounding & Agentic Engineering
- **Focus**: How to eliminate LLM hallucinations using deterministic repository context, structured schemas, and cross-IDE adapters.
- **Tone**: Deeply technical, code-first, reproducible benchmarks.
- **Example Topics**: Adapting `.cursorrules` for enterprise monorepos, prompt grounding vs fine-tuning.

---

## Pillar 2: Developer Velocity & Institutional Knowledge
- **Focus**: The quantifiable cost of tribal knowledge, documentation decay, and onboarding friction.
- **Tone**: Pragmatic, empirical, empathy for engineering leaders.
- **Example Topics**: Why PRDs belong in Git, measuring developer ramp metrics.

---

## Pillar 3: Sales Enablement for Technical Founders
- **Focus**: Turning complex technical products into structured battlecards, objection handling scripts, and discovery questions.
- **Tone**: Direct, actionable, playbook-driven.
- **Example Topics**: Cold email teardowns for CTOs, handling the "we'll build it internally" objection.

---

## Pillar 4: Open Source & Data Sovereignty
- **Focus**: Why critical company intellectual property and procedures must not be trapped in proprietary SaaS databases.
- **Tone**: Principled, developer-centric, privacy-conscious.
- **Example Topics**: Local-first architectures, avoiding SaaS vendor lock-in.
