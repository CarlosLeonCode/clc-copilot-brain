## 3. Core Messaging Pillars

### Pillar 1: Deterministic Single Source of Truth (SSOT)
- **Claim**: Institutional knowledge belongs in Git alongside your codebase, not trapped in proprietary cloud wikis.
- **Proof Point**: Markdown files, version control history, PR-based reviews, and CLI schema audits.

### Pillar 2: Universal Multi-IDE Copilot Grounding
- **Claim**: Stop your AI coding tools from guessing internal architecture and APIs.
- **Proof Point**: Automated symlinks for `.cursorrules`, `CLAUDE.md`, and `.windsurfrules` that pull directly from `AGENTS.md`.

### Pillar 3: Zero Vendor Lock-in & Open Governance
- **Claim**: Total data sovereignty with zero SaaS seat licensing taxes.
- **Proof Point**: MIT-licensed CLI, modular area directories, pure standard Node.js execution.
