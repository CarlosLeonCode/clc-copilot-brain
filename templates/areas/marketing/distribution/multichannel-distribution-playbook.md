# Multichannel Distribution Playbook

## Channel Strategy & Execution Cadence

### 1. Developer Communities (Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub)
- **Rule**: Pure value, zero promotional gating. Share complete tutorials, open-source repos, or reproducible architectural post-mortems.
- **Timing**: Tuesday or Wednesday morning (8 AM – 10 AM EST).
- **Format**: Text submission or link with detailed first-comment context.

---

### 2. LinkedIn (Technical & Executive Buyers)
- **Rule**: Hook within first 2 lines; breakdown the problem before offering the framework.
- **Frequency**: 2–3 posts per week from team founders and engineering leads.
- **Format**: Plain text with code screenshots, carousel teardowns, or short diagram breakdowns.

---

### 3. Technical Newsletter (Weekly Deep-Dive)
- **Audience**: Subscribed engineering managers, tech leads, and RevOps professionals.
- **Structure**:
  1. The Operational Bottleneck of the Week.
  2. The Second Brain Solution (Markdown template + Schema).
  3. Actionable PR/commit to apply this week.

---

### 4. Direct Outbound (Sales & Marketing Alignment)
- Align marketing content releases with sales outbound sequences in `sales/scripts/`.
- Every published case study must be weaponized into a 3-sentence sales snippet within 24 hours of publication.
