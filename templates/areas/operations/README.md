# Operations & Infrastructure Hub

Welcome to the Operations area of the Organizational Second Brain. This directory houses standard operating procedures (SOPs), internal tooling registries, and third-party vendor contracts.

## Structure
- [sops/](./sops/): Schema-compliant Standard Operating Procedures with executable checklists.
- [tools/](./tools/): Internal tooling catalog, assigned owners, licensing costs, and access procedures.
- [vendors/](./vendors/): Vendor management records, SLAs, and primary points of contact.
